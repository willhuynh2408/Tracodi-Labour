# Guidelines.md — Tracodi Labour / Labor Export Website

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the visual, structural, content, and interaction guidelines for the **Tracodi** website. The goal is to present a labor export company as **trustworthy, premium, structured, compliant, and internationally capable**.

The site should feel:
- Professional, not flashy
- Modern, but operationally serious
- Human-centered, while still system-driven
- Premium through spacing, typography, and clarity
- Reliable for both employers and workers

---

## 2. Brand Positioning
### Core brand idea
**Structured pathways from local talent to Labour job sites.**

### Brand personality
- Precise
- Disciplined
- International
- Reliable
- Calm under pressure
- Human but not overly emotional

### Messaging direction
The website must communicate that the company is not just a recruiter, but a **complete labor mobility partner**. Every message should reinforce:
- legal and compliant operations
- transparent recruitment and deployment process
- international market reach
- worker preparation and support
- employer confidence and repeat hiring value

---

## 3. Audience
The website serves two primary audiences:

### 3.1 Employers
Need confidence in:
- candidate quality
- recruitment pipeline visibility
- documentation accuracy
- compliance handling
- repeat hiring efficiency

### 3.2 Workers / Applicants
Need confidence in:
- clear job opportunities abroad
- fair and transparent process
- training and language preparation
- visa and embassy guidance
- post-arrival support

All sections should balance these two user journeys.

---

## 4. Visual Direction
### Overall look
The design language should feel like a blend of:
- premium corporate landing page
- international mobility platform
- workforce operations dashboard

### Visual principles
- Use strong spacing to create confidence
- Use contrast to highlight authority and clarity
- Use soft surfaces and gradients to avoid a harsh industrial feel
- Use imagery that shows professionalism, training, mobility, and real work environments
- Avoid clutter, over-decoration, and playful startup visuals

- **Body:** Inter

### Typography intent
- Headlines should feel strong, premium, and decisive
- Body copy should feel readable, calm, and modern
- Eyebrow labels should look operational and structured

### Type rules
#### Headlines
- Use bold to extra-bold weights
- Tight letter spacing for hero and section titles
- Large scale for main headline sections
- Keep headlines short and forceful

#### Body text
- Use medium line-height
- Prefer clean, complete sentences
- Avoid overly long paragraphs in cards
- Keep tone direct and informative

#### Eyebrow labels
- Uppercase
- High letter spacing
- Small size
- Used to organize sections and create hierarchy

---

## 7. Spacing and Layout
### Layout width
- Primary content container should stay within a **max-width around 7xl**
- Maintain generous horizontal padding on all screen sizes

### Spacing feel
Spacing is a major part of the brand. The interface should feel:
- breathable
- deliberate
- high-end
- easy to scan

### Section rhythm
Each major section should include:
1. Eyebrow label
2. Strong headline
3. Supporting paragraph
4. Structured cards, metrics, or media

### Grid usage
Use grids for:
- hero split layouts
- market cards
- process steps
- training tracks
- proof blocks

Avoid dense multi-column text layouts without visual breaks.

## 9. Component Guidelines
### 9.1 Navigation bar
The navigation should be:
- fixed at the top
- semi-transparent / glass style
- compact but premium
- easy to scan

#### Includes
- logo Tracodi Labour / wordmark
- menu links
- primary CTA button
- mobile menu toggle

#### Behavior
- Desktop: horizontal menu + CTA
- Mobile: hidden menu revealed by toggle

### 9.2 Hero section
The hero must establish trust immediately.

#### Required elements
- large, high-impact headline
- supporting paragraph
- primary and secondary CTA
- 3 authority stats
- right-side operational panel or trust summary
- strong full-width background image with dark overlay

#### Tone
The hero should feel like a **Labour operations brand**, not a generic local recruitment company.

### 9.3 Stats blocks
Stats should be used to signal authority.

Examples:
- workers deployed
- destination markets
- retention rate
- satisfaction score
- training tracks

Rules:
- keep numbers large and bold
- label them with small uppercase captions
- use sparingly and only for meaningful proof

### 9.4 Cards
Card-based layout is central to the interface.

#### Card types used in this design
- market cards
- flow cards
- track cards
- proof cards
- stat blocks
- side panels

#### Card behavior
- subtle lift on hover
- clear padding
- clear heading hierarchy
- readable copy
- no excessive ornament

### 9.5 Call-to-action buttons
#### Primary CTA
- gradient red background
- white text
- strong, directive wording
- slight upward motion on hover

#### Secondary CTA
- softer style
- can use glass/outline/light surface treatment
- supports the primary CTA without competing with it

CTA language should sound professional and action-oriented.

Examples:
- Request Proposal
- Talk To Our Mobility Team
- See Deployment Process
- Request A Design Consultation

## 10. Content Guidelines
### General content tone
Write in a tone that is:
- confident
- professional
- reassuring
- internationally oriented
- easy to understand
- slightly premium

Avoid:
- slang
- exaggerated promises
- emotional over-selling
- vague corporate clichés
- informal sales language

### Writing rules
- Use short to medium sentence length
- Be specific about process and value
- Focus on clarity over ornament
- Emphasize compliance, structure, readiness, and support
- Write like a serious international service provider

### Preferred messaging themes
- Labour opportunity
- structured deployment
- compliant operations
- worker readiness
- employer continuity
- training and transition support
- documented process
- international labor mobility

## 12. Market Content Guideline
For the “International Markets” section, avoid generic phrases like “We cooperate with many countries around the world.”

Instead, write with:
- credibility
- specificity
- international seriousness
- opportunity + trust balance

### Approved direction
Use content that emphasizes:
- reputable international partners
- real labor demand
- stable jobs and income potential
- professional work environments
- market-specific selection and matching
- long-term career development for workers

### Example style
> We cooperate with trusted partners across major international labor markets, creating access to stable employment, competitive income, and professional working environments for Vietnamese workers. Each program is carefully selected to match workforce capability, employer demand, and long-term development potential.

## 14. Motion and Interaction
### Motion style
Animation should be subtle and purposeful.

### Approved interactions
- gentle card lift on hover
- small CTA elevation on hover
- soft menu interactions
- pulsing market nodes for network visuals
- smooth scroll between sections

### Avoid
- bouncy animations
- flashy transitions
- complex motion that distracts from trust

---

## 15. Accessibility Guidelines
- Maintain strong color contrast, especially on red and dark backgrounds
- Ensure text remains readable over hero imagery using overlays
- Use semantic headings in proper order
- Provide descriptive alt text for all important images
- Ensure CTA buttons are clear and keyboard accessible
- Mobile navigation must be easy to open and scan

---

## 16. Responsive Design Rules
### Mobile priorities
- keep the hero readable without crowding
- stack grids into single columns when necessary
- maintain CTA visibility
- ensure text blocks do not become too wide or too dense

### Tablet and desktop priorities
- preserve premium spacing
- use multi-column layouts for authority and scanability
- maintain clear visual balance between text and cards/media

## 18. Do / Don’t Summary
### Do
- Design for trust first
- Keep the interface structured and premium
- Write with clarity and confidence
- Emphasize compliance and worker support
- Use strong spacing and disciplined card layouts
- Show Labour capability through specific market and sector logic

### Don’t
- Use generic recruitment language
- Overcrowd sections
- Overuse red
- Make the site feel playful or trendy
- Add unnecessary decorative elements
- Write vague claims without operational meaning

---

## 19. Final Standard
Every page, section, and content block should answer this question:

**Does this feel like a serious international labor mobility company that workers and employers can trust?**

If the answer is not clearly yes, revise the design, content, or structure until it does.
